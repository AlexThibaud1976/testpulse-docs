TestPulse builds reports **in your browser**. It has no TestPulse backend that receives or analyses your project data. Data does travel between the browser and Azure DevOps; your export and notification actions can also take it outside that environment.

## Where your data lives

| Data | Storage and visibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Reports and personal history** | Azure DevOps **Extension Data**, per user across the organisation. |
| **Shared reports** | Copy of the report in project-level Extension Data when you choose **Share to project**. |
| **Preferences and settings** | Extension Data, at user or project scope depending on the setting; some visual-only choices are stored in the browser. |
| **Repository and Cockpit snapshots** | Extension-owned data in Azure DevOps; repository organisation and shared tracking data do not modify test results. |
| **Exploratory sessions** | Documents and index in project-level Extension Data; automatic saving during the session. |
| **Explore captures and files** | Attachments uploaded to Azure DevOps, then linked to work items through your creation or closure actions. |
| **Report images** | Loaded from Azure DevOps and cached in memory; removed from the history snapshot to respect its quota, then read again if needed. |
| **Exports** | Files downloaded to your device. They then follow the storage and sharing rules of that device and your recipients. |
| **Notifications** | Message sent from the browser to the configured webhook URL when you choose **Test** or **Notify**. |

<Warning>
Project scope for sessions is not a confidentiality boundary between teams: session documents are shared at project level. The hub's **Tester** filter helps you find sessions; it is not an access control. Restricting active-session resumption to its author does not make its content private. Avoid entering information that must not be accessible to project members.
</Warning>

## What leaves through exports or notifications

A PDF, Word document, Excel workbook, HTML or PowerPoint presentation becomes a **local file**. TestPulse no longer controls its distribution once you share it.

For Teams or Slack, a report message contains its **title**, **environment**, **pass rate**, **execution rate** and **bug count**. The **Test** button sends a sample message. The destination is the URL saved in your settings; the content is not sent to a TestPulse server. See [Notifications](/en/collaboration/notifications-and-scheduling).

## Telemetry and external services

TestPulse does not include an analytics backend collecting your reports. Product data access uses Azure DevOps, and notifications you trigger use your webhooks. This describes TestPulse; it does not replace the policies of Azure DevOps, Teams, Slack or your browser.

## Azure DevOps Server

Azure DevOps data is read and saved against your server. Notifications remain outbound browser requests: network policy and CORS can prevent them. TestPulse then offers the payload to copy for manual handling. Local exports remain available.
