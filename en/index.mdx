## Results need interpretation

A grade and verdict summarise rules applied to the available data. They do not replace the team’s decision: check the scope, result freshness and missing information. TestPulse **does not run your tests**.

<Note>
  TestPulse runs in your browser, without a TestPulse backend. Depending on the action, it reads or writes in Azure DevOps, saves information in Extension Data, produces local files or sends a notification to a configured webhook. See the [action matrix](/en/concepts/read-only) and [privacy guidance](/en/security/privacy).
</Note>

The documentation covers Azure DevOps Services and the Server specifics described in the [installation guide](/en/getting-started/installation). Workshop screenshots are captured on Services; your layout may vary with version, permissions and configuration.
